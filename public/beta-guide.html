01 — First Login
Step by step
First Login &
Onboarding
01
Open your invite email

You received an email from HahaHub with your login credentials — email address and password. Keep it handy.

02
Go to hahahub.art → Tickle In

Click "TICKLE IN →" in the top right corner of the page. Enter your email and the password from the invite email.

💡 You can change your password later in My Hub → My Profile.

03
Welcome Wizard — Who are you?

On your first login, a 2-step wizard appears. Step 1: choose your role — Buyer (you license shows), Seller (you offer shows), or Both. This helps the platform show you the right things first.

04
Welcome Wizard — What's your first move?

Step 2: choose your first action — Browse Catalog, Upload a Show, or Check LaffWire. Click and you land directly on the right page. The wizard appears only once.

05
Navigation

Top menu: CATALOG (browse shows) · LAFFWIRE (industry feed) · MY HUB (your dashboard) · SHOWLOAD (upload a show). Your name and plan badge are top right.

Your Plan Badge

Under your name in the top right you'll see your plan: GIGL (free), LAFF, or ROAR. Beta users typically get LAFF or ROAR access.

FOUNDING Badge

As a beta user, you'll have a special FOUNDING badge on your profile — permanently. It marks you as one of the first producers on the platform.

Important: If the page loads slowly on your first visit — wait up to 20 seconds. The server wakes up from sleep mode. This will be fixed before public launch (Supabase Pro upgrade).

02 — Showload
Listing your show
Showload —
Upload Your Show

Click SHOWLOAD in the top menu. The form has 6 sections. You don't need to fill everything — starred fields (*) are required. You can edit everything later in My Hub.

00. Basic Info

Title (+ English title if not in English), author, genre, language, humor type, synopsis in English. Trailer URL if you have one.

01. Production

Cast size, duration, stage type, technical complexity, tech staff needed, touring friendly, director info.

02. Full Punch

Enable the Full Punch package here — see page 6 for full details. Also: music author, video info, script availability.

03. Market Performance

Premiere date & location, number of performances, total audience, where it's been played, awards.

04. Rights & Identity

Copyright holder, rights status (Available / Licensed), license type, territories, exclusivity.

05. Licensing Packages

Script-only license — set royalty % and optional advance fee. Full Punch pricing is set in section 02.

Show Poster

Upload an A4 ratio image (portrait 2:3). This is your main visual in the catalog. High resolution recommended — it's your first impression.

Production Photos

Upload up to 3 photos from the production. They appear in your show dossier. Buyers want to see the real thing.

→
Click "SHOWLOAD IT →" to publish

Your show appears in the catalog immediately. You can edit it anytime in My Hub → My Shows → Manage / Edit.

💡 GIGL (free) plan allows 1 show. LAFF allows 5. ROAR is unlimited.

03 — Catalog & Tickle
Finding & contacting shows
Catalog &
Tickle
01
Browse the Catalog

Go to CATALOG in the top menu. Shows are displayed as poster cards. Use filters on the left: genre, language, humor type, cast size, touring friendly.

💡 Miss Stefunny search (top of catalog) understands natural language — try "funny show for 3 actors, no video".

02
Open a Show Dossier

Click any show card to open the full dossier. It contains: Synopsis, Rights & Identity, Creative Engine (cast, director), Full Punch package details, Technical Stack, Market Performance, and the Select Package section at the bottom.

03
Add to Tickle List

See the hand icon (☝) on each show card — click it to save the show to your Tickle List (shortlist). Access your list in My Hub. You're not committing to anything yet.

04
Select a Package & Tickle

In the dossier, scroll to the bottom. Choose: Script (script only) or Full Punch (complete know-how). Click the Tickle button. A form opens.

05
Fill in the Inquiry Form

Your name and email are pre-filled. Write a message to the rights holder — introduce yourself, your theatre, what interests you. You can attach a PDF (company profile, etc.). Click TICKLE IT →.

💡 You'll receive a confirmation email. The rights holder is notified immediately.

Download Dossier PDF

In every show dossier, there's a "THE DOSSIER" button at the bottom. Click it to download a full PDF of the show — useful for internal review or sharing with your team.

04–05 — Pipeline & My Hub
04 — Deal management
Pipeline

Go to My Hub → Pipeline tab (or direct link in nav). Two views: TICKLED (inquiries you received) and TICKLER (inquiries you sent).

New → Contacted → Negotiating → Contract Sent → Signed → Royalties → Completed
Messages

Click any deal to open the message thread. Send text, files, or PDFs directly in the deal. Real-time — no email needed.

Change Status

Click the status dropdown in the deal header to move it forward. When you reach Signed, fill in deal terms — royalty %, territory, dates.

05 — Producer dashboard
My Hub

Click MY HUB in the top menu. Six tabs across the top:

Pipeline

All your deals in one place. TICKLED + TICKLER views.

Royalties

Log performances. System calculates royalties owed. Rights holders see it instantly.

Incoming

All inquiries you've received as a seller. Quick overview with status.

Analytics

Views, inquiries, and performance data for your listed shows.

Contracts

Signed deal summaries — territory, royalty %, dates, all in one place.

Calculator

Estimate royalty earnings based on ticket price and performances.

Edit Your Shows

In My Hub, scroll down to My Shows. Click Manage / Edit on any show to update all 6 sections — including Full Punch package, photos, pricing, and rights info.

06 — Full Punch
The franchise package
🥊 Full Punch —
Complete Know-How

Full Punch is the complete production package — everything a buyer needs to produce your show without a single phone call. Think of it as a franchise model: the buyer gets not just the script, but the entire production know-how.

What's in the package

✓ 📄 The Script always included
○ 📋 The Playbook director's production notes
○ 🎵 The Soundtrack original music files + cue sheet
○ 🎬 The Visuals video projection files + specs
○ 👗 The Wardrobe costume design + supplier list
○ 🏗️ The Set Blueprint set design plans
○ 🔧 The Tech Rider full technical requirements
○ 📸 The Promo Kit press photos + marketing assets
○ 🤝 The Handover Session live session with your team

✓ = always included · ○ = you choose what to offer

How to set it up

01
Go to Showload or Edit Show

Section 02. Full Punch — enable the checkbox "Offer Full Punch Package".

02
Set your royalty % and advance fee

Typically 15% for Full Punch vs 10% for script only. Advance fee is optional.

03
Check what you're including

Tick the elements you actually have prepared. Buyers see exactly what they get — no surprises.

04
Set Punch Language & Support

Language of all documentation (EN, SI, DE...). Enable Punch Support if your team is available for buyer onboarding.

What buyers see

In the catalog dossier, buyers see a Full Punch card with all your checked elements, language, and whether Punch Support is available — before they even send an inquiry.
