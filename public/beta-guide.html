02 — Showload
Listing your show
Showload — Upload Your Show

Click SHOWLOAD in the top menu. The form has 6 sections. Starred fields (*) are required. You can edit everything later in My Hub.

00. Basic Info

Title (+ English title), author, genre, language, humor type, synopsis in English. Trailer URL optional.

01. Production

Cast size, duration, stage type, tech complexity, staff needed, touring friendly, director info.

02. Full Punch ★

Enable the Full Punch package + set royalty %. Check elements you have. Also: music author, video info, script availability. See page 6.

03. Market Performance

Premiere date & location, performances, total audience, where it's been played, awards.

04. Rights & Identity

Copyright holder, rights status (Available/Licensed), license type, territories, exclusivity level.

05. Licensing Packages

Script-only license — set royalty % and optional advance fee. Full Punch pricing is set in section 02.

Show Poster

Upload A4 ratio image (portrait 2:3). High resolution recommended — it's your first impression in the catalog.

Production Photos

Up to 3 photos from the production. Appear in your dossier. Buyers want to see the real thing.

→
Click "Showload It →" to publish

Your show is live immediately. Edit anytime: My Hub → My Shows → Manage / Edit. GIGL: 1 show · LAFF: 5 · ROAR: unlimited

03 — Catalog & Tickle
Finding & contacting shows
Catalog & Tickle
01
Browse the Catalog

Go to CATALOG. Use filters: genre, language, humor type, cast size, touring. Miss Stefunny AI search understands natural language — try "funny show for 3 actors, no video".

02
Open a Show Dossier

Click any show card. Sections: Synopsis · Rights & Identity · Creative Engine (cast, director) · Full Punch details · Technical Stack · Market Performance. Download PDF: "THE DOSSIER" button at bottom.

03
Tickle List

Click the ☝ hand icon on any show card to save to your shortlist. Access in My Hub. No commitment yet.

04
Select Package & Tickle

In the dossier, scroll to bottom. Choose: Script (script only) or Full Punch (complete know-how). Click the Tickle button.

05
Fill in the Inquiry Form

Write a message to the rights holder. Attach a PDF if needed (company profile, etc.). Click TICKLE IT →. You get a confirmation email. Rights holder notified immediately.

💡 Track inquiries: My Hub → Pipeline → TICKLER

04 — Pipeline
Deal management
Pipeline

Go to My Hub → Pipeline tab. Two views: TICKLED (received as seller) · TICKLER (sent as buyer). Click any deal row to open messages & details.

New →Contacted →Negotiating →Contract Sent →Signed ✓ →Royalties →Completed
→ OPEN / ✕ CLOSE

Each deal row shows an arrow on the right. Click to open the message thread and deal details. Right panel shows HOW PIPELINE WORKS when no deal is open.

Messages & Files

Send text or attach files directly in the deal thread. Real-time updates — no email needed for negotiations.

Change Status

Status dropdown in the deal header. Move deals forward manually. At Signed — fill in deal terms (royalty %, territory, start/end dates).

Stats Bar

Top of Pipeline: New / Active / Signed counts + total royalties from all signed deals. Updates in real time.

Pro Tip — After Signing

When a deal reaches Signed status, go to My Hub → HAHAoffice and generate a proper licensing contract using the smart templates. Fill in the deal terms automatically.

06 — Full Punch
The complete know-how package
🥊 Full Punch

Full Punch = complete production package. Everything a buyer needs to produce your show without a single phone call. Think franchise: buyer gets not just the script, but the entire production know-how.

What's in the package

✓📄 The Scriptalways included
○📋 The Playbookdirector's production notes
○🎵 The Soundtrackmusic files + cue sheet
○🎬 The Visualsvideo projection files + specs
○👗 The Wardrobecostume design + supplier list
○🏗️ The Set Blueprintset design plans
○🔧 The Tech Riderfull technical requirements
○📸 The Promo Kitpress photos + marketing assets
○🤝 The Handover Sessionlive session with your team

✓ always · ○ you choose what to offer

How to set it up

01
Showload or Edit Show → Section 02

Enable "Offer Full Punch Package" checkbox.

02
Set royalty % + advance

Typically 15% Full Punch vs 10% script only.

03
Check your elements

Tick only what you actually have prepared. Buyers see exactly what they get.

04
Punch Language + Support

Language of docs. Enable Punch Support if your team is available for buyer onboarding.

What Buyers See

Full Punch card in dossier shows all checked elements + language + Punch Support availability — before they even send an inquiry.

08 — HAHAoffice & HAHAfriends
HAHAoffice 🧪 — beta feature
Contracts Management

My Hub → HAHAoffice. Your private contract workspace. Generate from templates, edit manually, save to cloud, download or print.

01
Choose a Template

3 HahaHub templates: Licensing Agreement · Co-production · Guest Performance. Click Use → to start. Or 📋 Copy to get an editable private copy.

02
Fill in Details — Live Preview

Form auto-detects all {{placeholders}}. Fill: show, counter party, royalty %, territory, dates. Preview updates as you type.

03
Save to HAHAoffice

Click 💾 Save Contract — goes to your private contracts list (only you see it). Status: Draft → Sent → Signed → Expired.

04
Edit Manually

Click ✏️ Edit on any saved contract. Inline text editor opens — type freely. ↩ Restore to undo changes. 📥 .txt or 🖨️ Print/PDF to export.

05
Create Your Own Template

Click + New Template. Write any contract text using {{party}}, {{show}}, {{royalty}}, {{territory}}, {{start_date}} etc. as placeholders. Saved privately.

HAHAfriends — your private network
Add from Profile

On any producer's profile page, click 🤝 Add to HAHAfriends. Their name, email, and plan are saved. Only you see your list.

Add Manually

My Hub → HAHAfriends → + Add Friend. Name, role, organisation, email, country, private notes.

Cloud Saved · Private

HAHAfriends saved in Supabase — accessible from any device. Nobody else can see your contacts or notes. Your private address book of the comedy world.
